Strategic infrastructure

Tank Storage Oman

Trafe Group of Companies acquired in April 2022 a tank storage facility of 9.000.000 BBL (almost 1.1 million MT) in the Port of Sohar, Sultanate of Oman.

The tanks (CTTT-C61, CTTT-C99, CTTT-22 and CTTT-90) are ready for EN590, D2, D6 and gasoline. Adjustments for A1 Jet Fuel storage are being completed.

⛴️ Berth & seaway: depth -19m at entrance, -16m at berth — capable of handling large tankers.
