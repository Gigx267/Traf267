Leadership Perspective

Message from our CEO

The idea behind Trafe leads back more than 20 years ago where a couple of Dutch no-nonsense sailors, who had already earned their spurs in offshore operations, decided to work together worldwide to carry out the most complex assignments—alone or in a team.

All educated and trained by intense experiences with high-pressure drilling in its most complex forms near the coast of the Netherlands and England in the dreaded North Sea. That sense of invincible seamanship is still the drive that all our employees radiate. Wherever they are. Whatever problem needs to be solved.

"Your problem is our challenge!"

Now that Trafe Inc. has grown into a globally operating enterprise with a massive database of specialists for every job, we no longer see each other every day. But the feeling remains of that group of friends who see every problem of the customer as an opportunity to perform. That is what Trafe Inc. stands for today.
