Welcome to Amanat construction & production oil LLC

Amanat construction & production oil, LLC Refinery (POCR LLP) is the largest enterprise in the northeast of Kazakhstan for oil refining and production of oil products and one of the three oil refineries of the Republic, 100% of shares are owned by “National Company “KazMunayGas” JSC.

Our Mision

To steadily developing enterprises of crude oil and gas production, petroleum refining, petrochemicals production, the tire-manufacturing complex, network of filling stations and services

Our Vision

Completely incubate customer directed meta-services after economically sound imperatives. Distinctively evolve diverse applications for petroleum enterprise processes and disintermediate functionalized solutions rather than progressive leadership.
